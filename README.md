# Finance Projects Portfolio

Welcome to my finance portfolio.

I am a first-year Engineering student with a strong interest in investment banking, equity research and financial analysis. This repository documents my self-directed learning through practical projects involving company valuation, financial modelling and Python-based market analysis.

## Featured Project

### NVIDIA vs AMD Equity Research

A comprehensive equity research project comparing NVIDIA and AMD to determine the stronger long-term investment opportunity.

**Project includes:**

- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation

- Moving average crossover trading strategy
- Final investment recommendation

**Skills Demonstrated**

- Equity Research

- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, yfinance)
- Technical Analysis
- Trading Strategy Backtesting
