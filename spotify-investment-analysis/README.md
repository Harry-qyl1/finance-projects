# Spotify Investment Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses Spotify as a potential investment opportunity.

### Week 1: Equity Research Report
- Business overview
- Subscriber growth analysis
- Competitive positioning
- Investment thesis

### Week 2: Revenue Forecast Model
- Historical revenue analysis
- Revenue growth assumptions
- Operating margin forecasts
- Upside and downside scenarios

## Key Takeaways
- Spotify remains the market leader in music streaming.
- Premium subscriber growth continues to support revenue expansion.
- Margin improvement may be driven by pricing power and monetisation of podcasts and audiobooks.
- Competition and royalty costs remain key risks.
