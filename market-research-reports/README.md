
# Aerospace Industry Report

## Objective
Evaluate the attractiveness of the global aerospace industry.

## Topics Covered
- Commercial aviation
- Defence spending
- Space industry
- Competitive landscape
- Industry risks and opportunities

## Companies Analysed
- Boeing
- Airbus
- Lockheed Martin
- RTX
- Northrop Grumman

## Key Conclusion
Long-term growth drivers remain favourable, although supply chain and geopolitical risks continue to pose challenges.
